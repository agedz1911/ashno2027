This Is Not Just Another Cadaveric Workshop.

How often have you attended a surgical workshop wishing for more hands-on time, closer faculty guidance, or the opportunity to explore a procedure that truly matters to your practice?

At ASHNO 2027, we are putting the dissection experience back into your hands.

Introducing an exclusive, XR-enhanced head and neck ablative dissection course, designed for surgeons who demand more from surgical education.

What Sets This Course Apart?

01 | SIX HOURS. DEDICATED TO DISSECTION.

No lengthy lectures consuming your laboratory time.

Our didactic teaching is delivered separately via Zoom before the course, allowing you to arrive prepared and devote six hours to hands-on anatomical exploration.

02 | ONE INSTRUCTOR. TWO DISSECTORS. NO CROWDED TABLES.

Experience focused, personalised guidance from experienced national and international surgical faculty.

With a maximum of two dissectors per table and one dedicated instructor, you will have meaningful opportunities to dissect, ask questions, receive feedback, and explore complex surgical anatomy.

03 | YOUR PROCEDURES. YOUR PRIORITIES.

Why follow the same programme as everyone else?

Choose from an extensive menu of ablative head and neck procedures tailored to your learning interests. Explore procedures including:

  • Midfacial degloving
  • Maxillectomy
  • Parotidectomy with infratemporal fossa dissection
  • Parapharyngeal space exploration
  • Thyroidectomy
  • Central compartment dissection
  • Neck dissection
  • Mandibulectomy
  • Glossectomy via pull through
  • Oropharyngectomy
  • Partial / total laryngectomy

Explore more than 6 procedures where time and anatomical feasibility permit.

04 | FRESH CADAVER. IMMERSIVE XR. REAL ANATOMICAL EXPLORATION.

Experience fresh cadaveric dissection complemented by extended reality (XR) technology.

Bridge three-dimensional anatomical visualisation with real surgical exposure to deepen your understanding of complex head and neck anatomy.

05 | LEARN WHAT TEXTBOOKS CANNOT FULLY TEACH.

Go beyond memorising operative steps.

Learn directly from experienced surgeons as they share:

  • • Surgical pearls and technical refinements
  • • Critical anatomical landmarks and danger zones
  • • Common pitfalls and strategies to avoid them
  • • Approaches to difficult surgical exposure
  • • Intraoperative decision-making and practical lessons from experience
