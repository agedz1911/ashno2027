WELCOME MESSAGE

From the President of PERDOKLI

Ladies, and gentlemen,

A very warm welcome to Surabaya, Indonesia.

It is a profound honor and privilege for me, on behalf of the Indonesian Society of Head and Neck Oncology (PERDOKLI), to welcome you all to the 10th Conference of the Asian Society of Head & Neck Oncology (ASHNO), held in conjunction with The 1st Surabaya Head & Neck Oncology Summit (SHNOS).

This milestone gathering brings together leading experts, clinicians, researchers, and healthcare professionals from across Asia and around the globe. Under our theme, “Sea to Summit: Innovating, Bridging Gaps & Driving Change,” we are reminded of our shared responsibility to continuously advance patient care in head and neck oncology.

Surabaya, known as the Heroic City of Indonesia, serves as the perfect backdrop for this summit — a place that embodies resilience, dedication, and progress. I encourage all delegates to not only immerse yourselves in the rich academic programs, interactive workshops, and discussions, but also to experience the vibrant culture and warm hospitality of East Java.

Dr. Marlinda Adham, Sp.THT-BKL, Subsp.Onk(K)

PRESIDENT, PERDOKLI / IHNOS
